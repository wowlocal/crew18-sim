# Podlodka Dive Privacy Information

Updated 17 September 2026.

Podlodka Dive is a single-player game. No registration or sign-in is required. The current app has no advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or tracking.

Progress, saved expeditions, settings, game journals, and captain notes are stored locally in the app container. The game does not upload them to a developer-operated server. Device backups may include app data according to your iOS settings.

Microphone and speech recognition permissions are optional and used when you choose to record a captain note. Speech recognition requires on-device support; there is no cloud recognition fallback. You can revoke permissions in iOS Settings.

Optional local notifications remind you about a saved expedition. They are scheduled on your device. Declining notification permission does not prevent playing or restoring a saved game.

If you choose to export logs or send feedback, the selected information goes to your chosen recipient. Review its contents before sharing. TestFlight may share feedback and diagnostics with Apple and the developer under Apple's TestFlight terms; this is separate from the game itself.

Deleting the app removes its local container. Manage device backups through iOS. For privacy questions or support, use https://github.com/wowlocal/crew18-sim/issues. Do not post personal information or private notes in public issues.
